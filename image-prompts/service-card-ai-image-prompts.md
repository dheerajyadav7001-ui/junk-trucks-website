# Junk Trucks — AI Image Prompts for Service Cards

Six prompts, one per service card, written to paste directly into ChatGPT (DALL·E) or another image generator. Each is built from your actual brand: the white Ford pickup with the dark green (#012D19) and orange (#F2661C) wrap, the round "Junk Trucks" hard-hat mascot logo, "JUNK REMOVAL & HAULING" text, and the crew's dark green polo shirts. Style is kept consistent (photorealistic, natural daylight, Ottawa residential/commercial settings) so all six feel like one shoot, not six different stock photos.

**One heads-up before you generate:** AI image tools are unreliable at rendering small logo text and phone numbers cleanly — they often come out as garbled or misspelled lettering. Two ways to handle it:
1. Generate as written below, then just crop tighter so the truck's side panel/logo isn't a focal point (it doesn't need to be perfectly legible in a service card thumbnail anyway), or
2. Add "no visible logos or text, plain white truck door" to the prompt if you want to skip the logo issue entirely and keep the truck generic.

Regenerate 2-3 times per prompt (ChatGPT gives variations) and pick the cleanest result.
